S-box and Rcon

Why these tables matter

The S-box powers SubBytes, the inverse S-box reverses it, and Rcon supports key expansion.

To substitute a byte, split its two hex digits: the first digit chooses the row and the second digit chooses the column. Example: input 53 uses row 5, column 3, so the S-box output is the value at that cell.
